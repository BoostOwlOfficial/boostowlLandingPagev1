Open with one strong paragraph. Tell the reader what this post is about and why they should keep reading. Be specific. "Here's what we learned shipping our first product to a cricket ground in Hapur" is better than "Today we want to share some thoughts on product launches."

## Use H2 for major sections

Body text is just regular paragraphs. Markdown supports the usual things:

- **Bold** for emphasis on key terms.
- *Italics* sparingly, for titles or first mentions.
- `inline code` for product names, file paths, or short technical terms.
- [Links](https://example.com) to external pages, or [internal links](index.html#pricing) using relative paths.

### Use H3 for subsections

You can also have lists:

1. Numbered lists for steps in a sequence.
2. Bullet lists for things without order.
3. Don't overuse them — prose is usually better than a list of fragments.

> Use blockquotes for customer quotes, important callouts, or anything you want to visually pull out of the main flow.
